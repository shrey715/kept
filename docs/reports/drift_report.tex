\section{TL;DR}\label{tldr}

\textbf{10 drift · 5 watch · 11 stable} across the 26 registered
features, comparing 690,062 training rows with the 96,000 cases served
tonight (features as of 2026-10-03 06:00:00+00:00).

Two things are going on, and only one of them is a problem:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{The baseline is different by design.} Training rows are
  \emph{every past decision since 2016}; tonight is \emph{today's open
  book}. Open cases skew later-stage and bigger-balance, so delinquency
  features (days past due, balance at risk, days in bucket) and cohort
  rates look ``drifted'' just because it's a different slice of
  customers, not because customers changed.
\item
  \textbf{The real finding:} \texttt{payroll\_delay\_days},
  \texttt{pay\_change\_vs\_3m\_avg},
  \texttt{estimated\_monthly\_spare\_cash} are \textbf{missing for every
  training row} but filled for \textasciitilde20--25\% of tonight's
  cases. The payroll history only lines up with recent decisions, so the
  model never got to learn from them. They're the heart of Kept (payday,
  spare cash), so the next retrain should backfill them for past
  decisions. Until then the rules and Kept sizing use them; the model
  just doesn't.
\end{enumerate}

\section{How to read PSI}\label{how-to-read-psi}

PSI (population stability index) compares two distributions bin by bin:
Σ (live\% − train\%) × ln(live\% / train\%). Numbers are cut at the
training deciles, categories by value, and \textbf{missing values get
their own bin}, so a feature that suddenly goes blank shows up too.
Usual bank bands: \textbf{\textless{} 0.1 stable}, \textbf{0.1--0.25
watch}, \textbf{≥ 0.25 drift} (investigate, then retrain).

\section{What we do with this}\label{what-we-do-with-this}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Nightly:} \texttt{kept\ drift} runs after
  \texttt{kept\ features}; a drifted \emph{model input} that isn't
  explained by the open-book baseline is a reason to hold the new scores
  and retrain.
\item
  \textbf{Next retrain:} backfill the three payroll features for past
  decisions, then re-check that their PSI and missing share line up.
\item
  \textbf{Not a fairness check.} Drift says the population moved; the
  fairness report says whether groups get treated differently. Different
  questions.
\end{itemize}

\emph{Rebuild:} \texttt{uv\ run\ python\ scripts/drift\_report.py} (with
\texttt{kept\ serve} stopped).
