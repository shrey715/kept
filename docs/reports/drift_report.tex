\section{How to read PSI}

PSI (population stability index) compares two distributions bin by bin:
$\sum (\text{live\%} - \text{train\%}) \times \ln(\text{live\%} / \text{train\%})$. Numbers are cut at the training deciles,
categories by value, and \textbf{missing values get their own bin}, so a feature that suddenly fills in or goes blank shows up
too. We use the usual bank bands: \textbf{below 0.10 stable}, \textbf{0.10–0.25 watch}, \textbf{0.25 or more drift}
(investigate, then decide on a retrain).

\paragraph{Payroll features: fixed} \code{payroll\_delay\_days} and \code{pay\_change\_vs\_3m\_avg} used to come from the
deposit snapshot, which is dated after every training decision, so training left them blank. They now come from the dated
monthly salary feed (\code{salary\_credit\_history}, employer pay only), read as of the earlier of the as-of date and the feed's
last complete month (\path{dbt/models/features/account_features.sql}). The same definition runs for training and live, so
they're filled about equally (22\% and 24\%) and stable. Retrained with them, the challenger lost to the champion beyond noise,
so the gate kept the champion. \code{estimated\_monthly\_spare\_cash} still needs the snapshot's spend fields: live only.

\paragraph{Delinquency features: a different slice, as expected} \code{max\_dpd} (0.53), \code{balance\_at\_risk} (0.39) and
\code{days\_in\_bucket} (0.47) moved because tonight is the open book, not a stream of decisions. The median open case is 52 days
past due, against 29 at a training decision, and has \$437 at risk against \$135. \code{contacts\_last\_7d} (4.45) is an
artefact of the date: the release's contacts end on 28 September, so no case has one in the 7 days before 9 October.

\section{What we do with this}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item \textbf{Payroll in training: done.} Delay and pay change come from the dated salary feed; the retrain didn't beat the
champion, so it wasn't promoted. Spare cash needs dated spend: keep a copy of the deposit snapshot each night, then add it.
\item \textbf{Teach the check about cohorts.} Compare cohort rates to the last training value per cohort, with a tolerance
of a few points, instead of using decile PSI. Today's check cries wolf on them every night.
\item \textbf{Fill or drop the empty inputs.} The SetFit backfill only predicts hardship, so the promise fields can only come
from the LLM extraction, and 0 of the 67,039 rows in \code{features.text\_features} carry one. \code{promise\_intent} leaves the model's inputs at the next promoted
retrain (the live champion was trained with it, blank). A feature that's always blank is just noise in the registry.
\item \textbf{Keep the nightly gate.} \code{kept drift} runs after \code{kept features}. A drifted \emph{model input} that
none of the reasons above explains holds the new scores and pages the owner.
\end{enumerate}
